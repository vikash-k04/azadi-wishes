About Celebrate Independence Day

Celebrate Independence Day is a simple Independence Day celebration website that lets people create personalized wishes using their name, message and photo.

What we do

You can create a personalized Independence Day card and share it with friends and family through a unique link or WhatsApp.

Privacy-first experience

Photos are processed and compressed in your browser to keep the experience fast and lightweight. The GitHub Pages version does not require an account or login.

Our goal

The goal is simple: make it easy for people to celebrate India's Independence Day and spread messages of unity, freedom and pride.

Jai Hind!

Jai Hind. Made for celebrating India's spirit of freedom.
