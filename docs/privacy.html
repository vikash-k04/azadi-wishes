PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters 🔒

This Privacy Policy explains how Celebrate Independence Day handles information when you use this website.

Information you provide

You may enter information such as your name and a personalized message. You may also choose to upload a photo to create your card.

Photo processing

Photos uploaded through the GitHub Pages version are processed and compressed in your browser before being used in the personalized card.

Personalized share links

Your personalized wish is encoded into the shareable URL used by this static version of the website. Anyone who receives that link may be able to view the information included in the wish.

For this reason, do not include sensitive, confidential or private information in your name, message or uploaded photo.

Accounts

The GitHub Pages version does not require you to create an account or log in.

Third-party services

The website may use third-party services or links in the future, including advertising services. Their own privacy policies may apply when you interact with those services.

Advertising

Advertising is not currently active on this website. If advertising is introduced in the future, this policy may be updated to explain relevant advertising technologies and choices.

Photo privacy & Automatic deletion

Photos uploaded to create a personalized wish card are stored securely and used only to render your card for people who receive your unique wish link. Uploaded photos are automatically permanently deleted after 30 days to protect user privacy and maintain database hygiene.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features or services change.
